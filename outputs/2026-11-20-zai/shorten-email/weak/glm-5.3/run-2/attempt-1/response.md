What changed and why:

- **Subject line states the outcome and the deadline.** "Venue stuff (sorry this is long)" buried both. Dana now knows the news and the clock before opening it.
- **The one action item leads the email.** The Friday deadline was the last thing in a wall of text; now it's the first thing Dana reads.
- **Bullets instead of prose.** Three topics, three bullets — easy to scan and reply to point by point.
- **Cut the hedging and process detail.** The apologies for length, the missed-calls backstory — that filler is what made the original *feel* long, not the actual content. The facts all survived; the throat-clearing didn't.
- **Kept the tone friendly.** It's still clearly Sam and Dana, not a memo.
