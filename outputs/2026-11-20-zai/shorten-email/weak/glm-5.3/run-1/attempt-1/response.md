What changed and why:

- **Subject line now carries the news.** "Venue stuff (sorry this is long)" buries the headline and the apology undersells it. The date and the Friday deadline are the two things Dana should see in an inbox preview.
- **Cut the hedging.** Three separate apologies/apologia in the opener ("sorry this is long," "apologies in advance," "might not end up being that quick") slowed things down before any information arrived. The content itself is short once trimmed.
- **Made the deadline an explicit ask.** "If I don't hear back I'm planning to sign Friday" was easy to skim past. It's now framed as the one thing needing Dana's input, with the date attached.
- **Numbers into bullets.** Cost and capacity are the details Dana might want to reference later; bullets make them findable instead of woven into a paragraph.

The tone stays friendly — this reads like Sam, just with the throat-clearing removed.
