I drew the kitchen as an SVG image: **`cozy_kitchen.svg`**. Open it in a web browser to see it. There's no image renderer installed here, so I couldn't look at the finished picture myself. I only confirmed the file is valid, so some shapes may sit a little off from where I placed them.

**What's in the picture:**
- **Window:** the sun is coming up over the hills in a peach and blue sky, with a couple of birds. There are red gingham curtains and two potted herbs on the sill.
- **Light:** a soft beam of sunlight falls from the window onto the floor, with dust motes drifting slowly through it.
- **Walls:** sage-green cabinets, a cream tiled backsplash and a rail with a ladle, spatula, whisk and a copper pan. On the right are wooden shelves with jars, stacked plates, cookbooks, a blue teapot, mugs and a trailing plant. A wall clock above the window reads about 7:30.
- **Counter:** a loaf of bread on a cutting board, a French press, a heart mug of coffee with steam rising, and a bowl of oranges and an apple.
- **Stove:** a cream vintage stove with a red kettle steaming on top.
- **Floor:** terracotta and cream checkered tiles, a round red rug, and an orange tabby cat asleep on it with little "z Z"s floating up.

The steam, dust and "z"s are gently animated in browsers that support SVG animation.
